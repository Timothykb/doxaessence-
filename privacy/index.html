Joining our newsletter

We collect your first name, email address, your consent and the time and version of your signup consent. We use these details to send our newsletter, launch updates and a personalised welcome email. Our legal basis for these emails is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Signing up is optional. The form requires your first name and email address to complete your signup; we do not ask for a postal address or payment details.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, free of charge, by emailing info@doxaessence.com or using the unsubscribe-by-email link in our messages. This opens an email request that we process manually. We stop newsletter and launch emails once we process your request. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew consent.

Website security and contact requests

Our hosting and infrastructure providers process technical information, such as your IP address and request information, to deliver the website, limit abusive requests and protect the signup form. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website and responding to enquiries and privacy requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and compliance with legal obligations where applicable (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Who receives your information

Access is limited to authorised people managing Doxa Essence and the service providers needed to operate this website and newsletter:

  • Supabase: stores newsletter registrations, consent records and the welcome-email queue.

Processing outside the European Economic Area

European hosting does not mean that all processing stays in the European Economic Area (EEA). Supabase describes possible access or processing in Singapore and the United States for support and operations. Resend uses service providers in the United States. Cloudflare operates a global network, so website requests and technical data may be processed outside the EEA, including in the United States.

The providers’ published data processing terms include European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers that require these safeguards. These clauses impose contractual data protection duties on the recipient; they do not mean that the destination country has identical laws to the EEA. Cloudflare also describes reliance on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for transfers covered by its certification. You can consult the safeguards and current processing locations in Supabase’s transfer information, Supabase’s data processing terms, Resend’s data processing terms, Resend’s subprocessor list and Cloudflare’s data processing terms. Contact us if you would like information about the safeguards relevant to your data or a copy of the applicable safeguards.

How long we keep information

  • Newsletter profile: we keep your signup details while you remain subscribed. At least once a year, we review whether the list is still actively used and the information remains necessary. If we discontinue the newsletter, we remove profiles no longer needed.
  • After you unsubscribe: we remove your newsletter profile from our active subscriber records within 30 days of receiving your request. This includes the linked welcome-email queue entry. We also review retained newsletter recipient and delivery records and remove or anonymise information no longer needed.
  • Minimal opt-out record: where necessary, we retain only your email address, the date of your opt-out and the fact that you opted out, separately from the active mailing list. We use this only to respect your choice and prevent accidental re-addition, based on our legitimate interest in respecting opt-outs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We review the need for this record annually and delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose.
  • Enquiries and privacy requests: we keep correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and, where necessary, demonstrate how it was handled or meet a legal obligation. We review retained correspondence at least annually and remove information no longer needed.

Removal from active records does not necessarily erase an existing provider backup immediately. Residual copies are subject to the provider’s backup retention and deletion cycle and are not used to resume newsletter mailings. Technical security and delivery logs are subject to the relevant provider’s retention settings; information required for a specific security incident, dispute or legal obligation is retained only for as long as that purpose requires.
