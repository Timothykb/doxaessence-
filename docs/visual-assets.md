Real photographic optics, crisp bottle, art-directed imperfect stone. No additional slogans, no other logos, no human figures, no watermarks. This is a concept packaging image, not a real product.

## Collectie

Bestand: `public/images/doxa-collection.png`

Prompt:

Use case: product-mockup. One extremely wide horizontal triptych ecommerce product photograph divided into exactly 3 equal square panels, total aspect ratio 3:1 at 1536x512. Three concept perfumes for independent Doxa Essence brand. Each square panel has one full centered glass rectangular perfume bottle with a sculptural polished chrome cylindrical cap, front facing, ample breathing room, beautiful soft shadow on off-white (#f4f2ed) seamless backdrop. Consistent premium studio light and same bottle shape. Left panel: pale amber liquid and cream paper label with exact words 'DOXA' then 'ESSENCE' then 'SOLA'. Middle panel: smoked dark amber liquid cream label 'DOXA' then 'ESSENCE' then 'NOCTE'. Right panel: nearly clear pale green liquid cream label 'DOXA' then 'ESSENCE' then 'PURE'. Bottles fully within each square; same size, height 65% of panel. High-end tactile real glass photo, no headings or added text outside labels, no borders between panels, no hands, no decorations. This is provisional packaging concept photography for a private design preview.
