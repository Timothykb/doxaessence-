## Creative direction
A 16–18 second luxury product film, with the user's current clear Doxa Essence
bottle, black cap, cream/burgundy label and unchanged proportions. Photographic
realism, physically plausible reflections, restrained movement, shallow depth
of field. Deep botanical greens, warm cream light, burgundy brand accents.

Biblical grounding: Genesis 2:8–14 describes a garden with trees pleasing to the
eye and good for food, the tree of life and the tree of knowledge in its midst,
and a river watering the garden before dividing into four branches.
Source: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%202%3A8-14&version=NIV
Treat the visual as an artistic interpretation, not an exact historical depiction.
No claim that the forbidden fruit was an apple. No invented temple, gates,
statues, angels, magical glowing fruit or named species presented as scriptural fact.
Do not confuse the fragrance's botanical notes with a biblical inventory of Eden.

## Shot plan
1. 0–4s / LIGHT: macro view of clean water rippling near the glass base. Soft
   dawn highlights reveal the bottle through out-of-focus foliage. Slow lateral
   tracking, no spinning or morphing bottle.
2. 4–9s / LIFE: reveal the complete bottle resting naturally on a riverbank rock,
   in a fertile garden of fruit-bearing trees. Gentle leaf movement, moving water,
   soft sunlight. Focus settles onto the actual label.
3. 9–14s / EARTH: slow small camera arc around the stationary bottle. Realistic
   glass refraction, damp earth, natural stone and softly blurred trees.
4. 14–18s / HERO: frontal bottle hold, legible original label, ample negative
   space. Add brand typography in editing, not generated within the footage:
   DOXA ESSENCE / C1 — THE GENESIS / A story you can wear.
