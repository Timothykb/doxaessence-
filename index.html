ABOUT DOXA ESSENCE

The story behind
doxa essence

The idea for Doxa Essence first came to me in early 2025 as a simple thought. Over time, that thought grew into something more. Through God’s guidance, I felt called to take this idea further and bring Doxa Essence to life.

The name Doxa Essence reflects the heart of our brand. Doxa is the Greek word for glory, and Doxa Essence means the essence of God's glory. Through our fragrances, we want to share a reflection of His glory and honour the biblical stories that inspire us.

Every fragrance is inspired by stories and themes from the Bible. We bring together fragrance, faith, and meaning to create more than just perfume. We want to inspire conversations, connect people, and share the beauty and meaning of these stories in a new way.

Doxa Essence is for everyone who loves fragrance and wants to discover the story and meaning behind each scent.

Doxa Essence. A scent you can wear, a story you can share.
